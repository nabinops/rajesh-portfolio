About Me

The Person Behind the Story

Proud Nepali 🇳🇵

Hey! I'm Rajesh Tiwari, 25, from the beautiful hills of Nepal. Currently working as a hospitality professional in Dubai, UAE — delivering world-class guest experiences at a premium 5-star hotel.

Beyond the hotel corridors, I'm an active content creator — sharing my life, culture, and journey across TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook. My content is raw, real, and rooted in the authentic stories of Nepal, hospitality, and expat life in the Gulf.

This is my little corner of the internet — built to share my story, connect with amazing people, and remind myself and you that dreams really do come true.

Vision & Mission

Mero Vision

Yuwa Nepali haru ra duniyabhar ka manchhe haru lai authentic storytelling bata inspire garna chahanchhu. Himalayako goreto bata Dubai ko aakash samma ko mero yatra ek praman ho — ki sapat aafu ma chain ra dharma sakchha.

Sanskriti, sakharat, ra saccho manav sambandh ma jiund ek brand nirman garna chahanchhu.

Mero Mission

Har din haajir hunu — kaam ma ra online ma — ek matra uddeshya le: mulya dinu. Hospitality ma, har guest lai kotha ko sabha bhandaa mukhya manchhe feel garaaunu parchha.

Content ma, har katha imandaar ra utthaune hunu parchha. Badh, farkaaune, ra sikai kabhi band nagarne — jindagi jaha leos ni.
